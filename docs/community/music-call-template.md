We are inviting musicians and sound artists around the world to interpret **<Solar Term>**.

You don't need to imitate Chinese music. You don't need professional training.

**What does <this moment in the seasonal year> sound like to you?**

For Lichun, you might ask: “What does the beginning of spring sound like to you?” This is a creative question; it does not mean spring begins everywhere at the same time.

You can respond with music, field recording, electronic sound, traditional instruments, AI-assisted composition, soundscape, experimental audio, or just a sound idea.

**Tell us why this sound belongs to your interpretation of this solar term.** Share what it means where you live, including differences from the Chinese context.

我们邀请你用自己的声音表达，不要求“古风”。告诉我们：在哪里、谁创作、听到什么、为什么、如何制作。个人记忆可以是灵感；历史和传统归属请附来源。

Read the [simple contribution guide](music-contribution.md) for accepted audio formats, links, license choices and AI disclosure. A sound idea needs no audio. For a sketch or track, share a publicly accessible audio link; do not attach large WAV masters to the repository. Declare sources, permission and your human contribution. CC0, CC BY, CC BY-NC and custom licenses receive individual review.

Submission is not automatic acceptance, publication or featuring. Featured expressions carry a human curator's reason and do not represent an entire country. Your chosen name, location, contribution and license stay with your work. Please follow the [community guidelines](../../COMMUNITY_GUIDELINES.md).
